How it works.

Create a professional creator page with bio, audience stats, content style, niche, and social proof. It's your brand deal landing page — always working for you.

02
📦
List your packages

Package your influence into structured, priced deliverables. Story mentions, Reels, UGC packs, monthly retainers — clear enough that brands can buy with one click.

Brands find you, buy your package, submit their brief, and you deliver. Funds held in escrow and released when you complete the work. Zero back-and-forth.
